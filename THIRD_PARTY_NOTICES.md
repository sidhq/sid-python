# Third-party notices

The native `sid._snippet` extension statically links third-party Rust crates.
Their exact resolved versions and registry checksums are recorded in
`Cargo.lock`; release CI also emits CycloneDX SBOMs from the locked dependency
graph.

- [Alyze](https://github.com/turbopuffer/alyze), version 0.1.5 — MIT license.
- [PyO3](https://github.com/PyO3/pyo3), version 0.29.2 — Apache-2.0 or MIT license.

Transitive crates retain their respective copyright notices and license terms.
The source distributions supplied by crates.io and the generated release SBOM
are the authoritative inventory for a particular release artifact.
